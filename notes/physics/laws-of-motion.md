# Laws of Motion & Friction

## Basics

$$\vec F = \frac{d\vec p}{dt} = m\vec a \qquad \vec J = \vec F\,\Delta t = \Delta \vec p$$

## Friction

$$f_s \le \mu_s N \qquad f_k = \mu_k N \qquad \tan\theta_{\text{repose}} = \mu_s$$

Block on a rough incline sliding down:

$$a = g(\sin\theta - \mu\cos\theta)$$

## Connected bodies

**Atwood machine:**

$$a = \frac{(m_1 - m_2)\,g}{m_1 + m_2} \qquad T = \frac{2m_1 m_2\, g}{m_1 + m_2}$$

## Lift problems

- Accelerating up: $N = m(g + a)$
- Accelerating down: $N = m(g - a)$

## Non-inertial frames

Pseudo force on a mass $m$ in a frame with acceleration $\vec a_0$:

$$\vec F_{\text{pseudo}} = -m\vec a_0$$

Velocity $v$ of efflux:

$$v = \sqrt{\frac{2gh + \frac{2(P - P_0)}{\rho}}{1 - \left(\frac{a}{A}\right)^2}}$$
